Aquatic Retreat

Infinity Pool
& Sundeck

Suspended at the edge of a private bay, our infinity pool is one of the resort's defining icons. The water appears to dissolve into the horizon — a seamless meeting of turquoise and gold that shifts with the light from dawn to dusk.

Flanking the pool, the sundeck is furnished with hand-crafted teak loungers and private cabanas, each attended by a dedicated pool butler. Chilled towels, tropical refreshments, and the gentle sound of moving water — time unfolds here without urgency.

  • 60-metre infinity-edge design overlooking the bay
  • Private poolside cabanas with butler service
  • Heated to 30°C year-round for perfect immersion
  • Adjacent adults-only plunge pool and sun terrace
  • Poolside cocktail bar open from 10 am to sunset

Wellness & Healing

Veda Spa
& Wellness

Nestled within a lush garden pavilion scented with wild jasmine and sandalwood, the Veda Spa is a sanctuary within a sanctuary. Our philosophy draws equally from ancient Ayurvedic wisdom and contemporary wellness science to restore the balance of body, mind, and spirit.

Each of our sixty treatments is choreographed by master therapists who take time to understand your body's needs before the first touch. From Panchakarma cleansing rituals to signature hot-stone journeys, every session is a deeply personalised encounter.

  • 60+ treatments across Ayurveda, Swedish, and deep tissue
  • Private couples treatment rooms with garden views
  • Himalayan salt steam room and cold plunge therapy
  • Daily yoga and meditation at sunrise by the reflecting pool
  • Personalised wellness consultations with resident therapists

Culinary Arts

Signature Dining

Four distinct restaurants, each a world of its own — curated by our celebrated Executive Chef and rooted in the finest seasonal produce.

Fine Dining

The Coastal Tasting Room

An intimate twelve-table restaurant perched above the private bay. Our chef presents an ever-evolving seven-course tasting menu that celebrates the finest seafood and coastal produce — accompanied by an exceptional cellar of natural wines.

All-Day Dining

The Orchard Table

An open-air garden restaurant where morning light filters through the canopy onto tables set with hand-fired ceramics. Farm-to-table breakfasts, leisurely lunches, and botanical cocktails served from a living herb terrace make this a resort favourite.

Grill & Bar

Ember — Bar & Grill

Wood-fire cooking at its most refined. Our open grill restaurant specialises in sustainably sourced cuts, whole-roasted local fish, and artisanal flatbreads — served beneath the stars with hand-crafted cocktails drawn from our seasonal spirits collection.

& Private Lawns
