What is the resort's cancellation and amendment policy?

Standard individual reservations may be cancelled or modified with zero penalty up to 48 hours prior to arrival date. For bespoke banquet events, Grand Ballroom bookings, or private yacht charters, please consult your assigned event coordinator for contract specific terms.

Does the resort accommodate custom dietary restrictions and allergies?

Absolute precision is taken with dietary preferences. Our executive culinary team can tailor gluten-free, vegan, kosher, halal, or allergy-specific menus for both 24/7 in-suite dining and our fine dining restaurants. Simply specify your preferences in the contact form or during check-in.
