Curated Adventures

Resort Excursions

Step beyond the resort and discover the living world around us — guided by experts, shaped around your pace.

Marine

Private Reef Snorkelling

Guided by our resident marine biologist, explore the vibrant coral ecosystems of the protected bay. Equipment, boat transfers, and a floating champagne lunch are all included.

Duration: Half day · Departs 8:30 am

  • Premium snorkelling equipment included
  • Marine biologist guide throughout
  • Private boat, max 6 guests
  • Floating champagne lunch on the water
Nature

Jungle Canopy Walk

Traverse suspended walkways through the ancient jungle canopy with a naturalist guide. Encounter rare bird species, ancient trees, and the profound stillness of an undisturbed ecosystem.

Duration: 3 hours · Departs 6:00 am or 4:00 pm

  • Expert naturalist guide
  • Safety harness and equipment provided
  • Sunrise or golden-hour departure options
  • Post-walk jungle breakfast included
Water

Private Kayak Safari

Glide silently through mangrove waterways and sea caves only accessible by kayak. This private guided experience concludes with sunset cocktails on a secluded sandbar.

Duration: 4 hours · Afternoon & sunset slot

  • Premium sea kayak equipment
  • Expert guide, max 4 guests
  • Sandbar cocktail hour at sunset
  • Return transfer by resort boat
